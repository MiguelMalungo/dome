How it works

From kit to dome in four steps.

  1. 1

    Choose your model

    Three diameters (4, 6 and 8 m) and four covers. We help you choose for your site, your use and your budget.

  2. 2

    Receive the kit

    Easy-to-assemble wooden structure triangles and the cover panels: all pre-cut, numbered and grouped by ring, with connectors, fixings and an illustrated manual.

  3. 3

    Prepare the base

    A timber platform on pad footings or ground screws: less concrete, less impact on the soil. We design the base with you.

  4. 4

    Build ring by ring

    From the ground up, each ring locks the next. The manual shows where every triangle goes and we are a phone call away.

Sustainability

Build light, with a material that grows.

  • Wood stores carbon

    Trees take CO₂ out of the air and the wood keeps it stored for the whole life of the building: around one tonne per cubic metre.2

  • The most efficient shape

    A sphere encloses the most volume with the least surface. A D’OME has about 22 % less wall and roof exposed to cold and heat than a box-shaped house with the same floor area and volume.1

  • Little waste on site

    Triangles and panels arrive cut to size. There are almost no offcuts or rubble on your land.

  • Light foundations

    A timber platform on pad footings or ground screws uses far less concrete and leaves the soil almost untouched.

  • Take it apart, use it again

    All joints are bolted. The dome can be taken down, moved or extended, and the wood stays useful.

  • Responsibly sourced timber

    We work with timber from sustainably managed forests, certified FSC® or PEFC.

FAQ

Before you start.

Do I need planning permission for a dome?

It depends on the size, the use and the municipality. A home generally needs a building permit from the local council; small or removable structures may have simpler rules. We help prepare the technical documents for your application.

How long does it take to build?

It depends on the model, the cover and how many people help. The frame goes up quickly; the cover and finishes take the most time. Your quote includes an estimate for your project.

Which tools do I need?

A drill-driver, a stepladder, simple scaffolding for the upper rings, a level and safety gear. The full list is in the manual.

Is it comfortable in winter and summer?

With the insulated covers, OSB or cork, yes: less outside surface means less heat lost in winter and less gained in summer. The technical fabric is designed for seasonal use.

Can I add windows and doors, or combine covers?

Yes. Any triangle can be glazed and the entrance can be designed to order.

Where do you deliver?

Mainland Portugal. For other destinations, including the rest of Europe, get in touch.
